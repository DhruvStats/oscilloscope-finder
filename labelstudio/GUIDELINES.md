# Labelling guidelines - PoC oscilloscopes

Classes (one box per physical instrument, tight around the whole case incl. feet and knobs):

| Label | Instrument | How to recognise it |
|---|---|---|
| `rs_rtb2004` (key 1) | Rohde & Schwarz RTB2004 | light grey/blue case, large touchscreen, blue hexagon grille at the back |
| `tek_tds2014` (key 2) | Tektronix TDS 2014 | teal case; front has **coloured** channel buttons (yellow/blue/pink/green, red MATH) and **5 BNC inputs** (CH1-4 + EXT) |
| `tek_tds1002` (key 3) | Tektronix TDS 1002 | same teal case; front buttons all **grey**, **3 BNC inputs** (CH1, CH2, EXT); back has the RS232/Centronics/GPIB module |

Rules
- Box every target instrument you can see, even if partly hidden or cut by the photo edge. An unboxed
  target teaches the model "this is not a scope".
- Do not box other instruments (multimeters, power supplies, other scopes), monitors, sockets, signs.
- Back/side view and you cannot tell TDS 2014 from TDS 1002? Draw the box with your best guess and set
  status **unsure which model** - it will be checked against the session.
- Photo without any target: no boxes, status **no target oscilloscope** (useful negative example).
- Status **done** when finished. Boxes are pre-filled from the model / earlier review: fix, delete or add.
